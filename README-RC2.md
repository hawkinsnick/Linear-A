# Linear A Open Corpus — 0.4.0-rc2-local

RC2 is an exhaustion/reclassification pass over RC1's high-priority disputes.

## Main result
Several RC1 'conflicts' were badly framed rather than genuine binary epigraphic
choices. RC2 separates sign identity, compound notation, fractional notation,
damage/lacunae, segmentation, spatial layout and metrological interpretation.

The high-priority open queue falls from 15 to 9, but this must **not** be read as
six Bronze Age readings being proven. Most of the reduction comes from better
classification.

## New infrastructure
- source-neutral RC2 case register
- visual/form-based paleographic observations
- explicit sensitivity branches for unresolved readings
- updated unresolved queue

## Strong examples
- HT15: fraction/compound notation, not ordinary sign substitution.
- MA10b: independent scholarship documents both 404VAS+A and 412VAS structure.
- HT Zd 156: TA-JA followed by fraction K, not a lexical TAJAK boundary problem.
- PR Za 1: damaged/lacuna sequence before KE must precede segmentation claims.
- SY Zb 7: damage prevents treating it as a clean word-boundary dispute.
- KN Zc 6: actual-object photography exists; spatial layout must be represented
  separately from a forced linear word stream.

Nothing has been published to GitHub. No copyrighted plates/images or raw
third-party corpus payloads are bundled.
