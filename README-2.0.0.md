# Linear A Open Corpus 2.0

2.0 freezes the project's second research architecture: source-defined word structure,
adversarial positional testing, canonical-site sensitivity, cross-representation
replication, edge-alternation graphs, document-resampled robustness, contextual
conditioning, and explicit scholarly-interoperability rules.

## Evidence ladder

1. Source structure: explicit serialized SigLA Word objects.
2. Internal enrichment: positional signals under within-word nulls.
3. Adversarial robustness: type deduplication, leave-one-out and dominant-form ablation.
4. Geographic sensitivity: leave-one-canonical-site-out.
5. Cross-representation replication: separately encoded corpus with shared upstream ancestry.
6. Form-network evidence: attested edge-removal relations and a sign-order null.
7. Document-cluster robustness: resampling whole documents.

No rung automatically promotes a sign to an affix or a form relation to morphology.

## Open gates

- blind known-answer Linear B calibration;
- genuinely independent epigraphic replication;
- direct per-record raw find-place provenance export;
- primary-plate adjudication of remaining source conflicts;
- inferential administrative-context models;
- linguistic interpretation.

## Research culture

The project is designed to make disagreements inspectable without turning scholarship
into competition. Other researchers' claims retain attribution and their own
operational definitions. Our own mistakes remain fully correctable and versioned.
