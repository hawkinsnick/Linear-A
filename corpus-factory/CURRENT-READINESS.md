## How to interpret readiness

“Pre-expert maximum” is a scoped work-boundary label. It does not certify that all remaining work requires an expert. Source acquisition, permission, record joins, primary collation, calibration and expert adjudication remain distinct dependencies. Use the member’s current ledger and selected review packet before handing off a task.

Native fields that are absent or use another unit mean unknown or differently scoped; they must not become zero verified objects, zero unresolved issues, or inferred acceptance. Source-derived metadata and multiple dependent editions remain attributed assertions.

The [20 October audit](VALIDITY-AUDIT-2026-10-20.md) passed 163 scoped commands and checked 1,095 indexed artifact hashes at its recorded revisions. It did not establish empirical AI accuracy, independent source truth, browser execution or live Actions success. These historical check counts are not a live validation badge.
