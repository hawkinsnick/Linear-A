Use each corpus’s `scripts/attribution_review.py --case ID` to retrieve the finding, remaining gap and complete referenced native evidence together. `scripts/evidence_query.py --list attribution-review` lists its review tasks. SHA-256 changes block attribution queries until the evidence is inspected and the review refreshed.

The master keeps per-member integrity snapshots and source-routing instructions; it does not pool protected source content or infer cross-script equivalence. The Linear A GORILA refusal remains in force. Egyptian-Hieroglyphic-Corpus and LightroomIsSlow remain outside this18-member registry.

No priority is inferred merely from a large number of apparatus notes.
