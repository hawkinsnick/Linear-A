## Researcher workflow

1. Open the member's native review packet below. Choose a bounded question or target set; do not request blanket corpus approval.
2. Complete listed source/permission or source-editor prerequisites. Record the actual edition/file, page, line, figure and source version accessed. A missing source is not an expert decision.
3. Give the qualified reviewer the preserved source assertions, competing alternatives, uncertainty, dependence, rights and downstream effect. Use exact native IDs and revision-bound evidence.
4. Obtain a separately attributable response: identity/affiliation, relevant expertise, date, conflicts, source dependence, evidence actually consulted, per-target reasoning and scope, confidence, counterevidence and unresolved objections. Inconclusive or additional-source-required decisions are valid.
5. A human maintainer must verify identity, expertise, independence, source access, redistribution permission and adequacy under the member's native acceptance rules. Preserve the review evidence and exact input hashes. Passing a validator or filling a prepared request does not admit a reading.
