# Anatolian and Cypriot coverage expansion

The combined corpus integrates the complete existing Anatolian dossier layer and adds a newly acquired Cypriot syllabic catalogue discovery layer. Native documents and AI skills retain the evidence and rights boundaries.

| Layer | Before | Now | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Anatolian declared collection views | 19 observed IDs + 32 overlapping portable entries | 287 full digital-entry dossiers | Existing reconciled native metadata now accessible in the combined package; no new ancient readings |
| Cypriot syllabic Greek | 592 parsed IG entries | 592 parsed IG entries + 210 legacy squeeze identifiers | New source-bound geographic discovery/concordance rows; no new Greek admission |
| Combined package | 9,116 mixed rows | 9,562 mixed rows | Heterogeneous, overlapping views; unique inscription/object total unknown |

The Anatolian API and exporter previously read an enumeration register without an `entries` array, producing an unusable whole-register API response and empty export. They now expose the full dossier records losslessly; the browser selects those entry rows individually. Source lineage and unverified edition locators remain intact.

Cypriot identifiers come from the BBAW [IG XV Cyprus squeeze catalogue](https://ig.bbaw.de/de/abklatsche/ig-xv-cyprus), retrieved on 2026-10-12 through ordinary HTTP. The native snapshot records the response SHA-256 and observation time; only factual identifier/geographic metadata are retained publicly. All 210 selected rows were checked against the independently parsed HTML section. They include 138 Rantidi labels, four Paphus vetus labels and 37 Golgi labels. Fragment, cast and uncertain-site qualifiers are retained. Two Cyprus-alibi labels lack an explicit site.

The parsed IG population, XML quarantine, Greek subset, source-interpreted sign counts and calibration inputs are unchanged. Museum seeds are not automatic IG/object joins. BBAW catalogue availability grants no blanket license for its images or unpublished editions.
