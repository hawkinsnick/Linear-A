## Priorities

1. Make current native coverage and readiness unavoidable at the researcher entry point: catalogue identities, encoded readings, source-collated records and independently accepted judgments need separate denominators. Reconcile historical/current summaries and version labels without changing historical evidence.
2. Continue bounded source work: missing publication locators, direct editions, exact record joins and source-dependent competing readings. A source-access dependency is not an expert-only decision. GORILA work must remain within recorded restrictions.
3. Validate research usefulness with bounded specialist tasks and separately attributable reviewer decisions. For predictive or inferential claims, acquire the linked Linear B gold/graphical inputs and execute the frozen calibration before claiming accuracy. Test AI answer citation, abstention and uncertainty behavior empirically rather than treating instruction compliance as proven.

No new canonical reading, physical-object identity, source permission, expert acceptance or release-readiness promotion results from this audit. No newly confirmed software P0 was found within the replayed scope; this is not universal P0 certification.
