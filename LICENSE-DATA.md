# Data licensing note

The initial sign inventory is a factual metadata compilation derived from Unicode character metadata and GORILA-based identifiers. Unicode's licensing terms apply to Unicode-derived material.

This repository does **not** redistribute GORILA's published inscription transcriptions, George Douros's tabulations, or other third-party corpora merely because they are available online. Such material must be handled according to its applicable copyright/license terms.
