# 2.4.1 — Corpus expansion and source audit

## Purpose

2.4.1 expands the evidence architecture without changing the frozen 2.3/2.4 experimental hypotheses. It separates acquisition, rights, lineage, prospective validation, and later analytical use.

## Prospective SigLA cohort

The original validated local SigLA snapshot remains frozen at 802 decoded documents and 5,144 sign attestations. SigLA's public update log records four document labels added on 2026-05-21 and twenty on 2026-06-26. Their membership is frozen in `analysis/2.4.1_prospective_sigla_cohort.csv` before candidate-outcome inspection.

This cohort is not automatically independent epigraphic evidence. Each record must later receive source-lineage classification. The prospective claim is chronological: the project's candidate screens predate these SigLA additions.

## Evidence layers to add

The expansion schema targets: sign instances/palaeography; source-specific readings; scribe attributions; support/object type; period; site and finer findspot; physical side/region/line/layout; separators and damage; source lineage; image/tracing references and rights; and competing scholarly assertions.

No field becomes canonical merely because one digital project supplies it.

## Rights policy

SigLA states that its dataset and drawings are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Those obligations travel with SigLA-derived material. GORILA images/text and copyrighted scholarly commentary are not redistributed merely because another tool technically mirrors them. Mixed-source projects are audited field-by-field.

## External toolsets

Linear A Workbench, pyaegean, lineara.eu and lineara.xyz are comparison/interoperability targets, not sources to clone. Their useful engineering and analytical features will be benchmarked independently while their upstream rights and evidence lineages remain explicit.
