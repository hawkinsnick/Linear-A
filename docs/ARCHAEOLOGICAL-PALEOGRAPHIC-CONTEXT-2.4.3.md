# 2.4.3 — Archaeological and palaeographic context

2.4.3 defines context as source assertions rather than inferred truth. Scribe, support, period, site, find area, find spot, physical side/region/line, layout, damage, image and tracing references are represented through the 2.4.1 evidence-instance and 2.4.2 witness layers.

The existing `document_site_mapping.csv` remains a reviewed identifier-derived layer. It is not relabeled as direct excavation findspot metadata. Direct source joins may supersede individual proxy assertions only with provenance.

Palaeographic sign instances remain distinct from normalized sign identity. A drawing or crop may be referenced or redistributed only according to its source rights.
