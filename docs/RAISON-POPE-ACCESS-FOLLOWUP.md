- Private OCR and AI-assisted collation of a lawfully supplied copy, including whether hosted AI processing is permitted or processing must stay on local machines.

I’m happy to keep restricted components private and retain the license, attribution, correction notices and credit lines you specify. There is no need to authorize all four uses together. I would also appreciate details of any available digital or print copy, fees, and any separate permissions required from authors or image owners.

Thank you for helping me establish the right scope before using the edition.
