## Interpretation
Our differentiator is evidence lineage, falsifiability, claim auditing and multi-witness representation. Workbench/pyaegean currently have a major usability and API advantage. SigLA remains the palaeographic source authority in our architecture. We should close usability/interchange gaps without copying another project's implementation or overstating comparative superiority.
