# 1.0 reproducibility contract

1. Raw upstream SigLA payloads are not bundled.
2. Upstream acquisition remains opt-in and provenance/checksum tracked.
3. SigLA-derived records retain applicable upstream licensing.
4. Analysis never silently substitutes `f3` for explicit source-word membership.
5. Confidence filtering must break sequences rather than bridge across excluded signs.
6. Source-specific disagreements remain distinct observations until adjudicated.
7. Corrections are recorded; superseded models remain visible in research history.
8. Every headline result must identify its unit of analysis and null model.
9. Structural statistical evidence is not automatically linguistic evidence.
10. Blocked validation steps are reported as blocked, not approximated into success.
