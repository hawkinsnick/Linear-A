# Combined Corpus Research AI 1.0

A vendor-neutral research orchestration skill for the registered **corpus projects**.

It coordinates individual corpus skills without merging their scholarly data. A researcher can ask one-corpus or cross-corpus questions; the combined skill routes to native evidence, enforces comparability gates, preserves provenance and rights, and reports blocked inference rather than manufacturing conclusions.

## Nontechnical use
Provide your AI with this `combined-ai-skill` directory plus the AI bundles for the corpus projects you want to study. Tell it: **Use SKILL.md as the governing research instructions and registry/corpus-projects.json as the active corpus-project registry.**

Suggested first question: “Which corpus projects can address my question, what evidence can each provide, and what comparisons are scientifically permitted?”

## 1.0 guarantees
- no corpus duplication in the combined layer;
- individual corpus skills remain authoritative;
- explicit cross-corpus comparison gate;
- provenance/source-independence discipline;
- rights remain corpus/source-specific;
- staleness detection;
- adversarial conformance tests;
- extensible registry for future corpus projects.

Version 1.0 describes the orchestration contract and validation suite. It does not certify the completeness or scholarly correctness of every member corpus.
