# Researcher Quickstart

1. Give the AI this combined skill and the individual AI bundle(s) for the corpus projects relevant to your question.
2. Ask the research question in ordinary language. You do not need JSON, Python, Git, or specialist prompt syntax.
3. The AI should identify the relevant corpus projects before analysis.
4. For cross-corpus work, expect a comparability check before an inferential comparison.
5. Treat BLOCKED as a useful result: it identifies evidence or methodological work needed before the question can responsibly be answered.

## Useful prompts
- “Find all evidence relevant to X and distinguish observations from interpretations.”
- “Compare X across these corpus projects, but stop if the units are not comparable.”
- “Trace this claim back to its corpus record and source.”
- “What evidence would be required to move this blocked comparison forward?”
- “Reproduce this statistic and state every exclusion.”
- “Which parts of this answer rely on dependent sources?”

The combined skill should never require a researcher to know the repositories' internal layouts in order to ask these questions.
