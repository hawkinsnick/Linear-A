# Research contract

This AI layer inherits the Linear A Open Corpus evidence model.

## Evidence ladder

Keep these categories distinct:
1. physical/document observation;
2. source transcription or classification;
3. normalized representation;
4. computationally derived result;
5. scholarly interpretation or hypothesis;
6. AI-derived calculation or synthesis.

An answer may connect categories, but must not silently promote a lower-level observation or derived pattern into a linguistic conclusion.

## Source independence

Multiple digital representations can share an upstream edition or transcription. Do not count them as independent confirmation unless the repository's lineage evidence supports independence.

## Negative and blocked evidence

Blocked, null, failed, corrected and superseded results are evidence about project state. Do not omit them merely because they are inconvenient to a proposed hypothesis.

## Citability

Prefer stable corpus IDs and release/version identifiers. For a research answer, name the exact repository file(s) and record identifiers used. When possible include enough fields or calculation steps for another researcher to reproduce the result.

## Rights

Original project licensing does not overwrite upstream rights. Preserve record/source-specific licensing and attribution. Do not reproduce source material that the supplied rights metadata marks as non-redistributable.
